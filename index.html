I am an MSc candidate in Statistics at Simon Fraser University, advised by Dr. David Stenning. My research lies at the intersection of Bayesian inference, fast approximation methods, and scalable samplers. I am interested in their applications in astrophysics. My current project involves capturing the non-stationarity of stellar evolution tracks using Deep Gaussian Processes (Deep GPs). I am broadly interested in developing principled statistical methods that are computationally efficient and theoretically grounded.

I enjoy learning about faster and scalable algorithms with theoretical backing. Recently I've been learning about MCMC samplers, GPs and Deep GPs and am exploring their applications in astrostatistics.

Before my Master's, I completed a BSc in Statistics at the University of Delhi, India. Outside of research, I enjoy hiking, swimming, and teaching. I have dabbled a bit into cubing and some logical puzzles. I enjoy live music and my favourite thing to do is to read on a sunny day.
